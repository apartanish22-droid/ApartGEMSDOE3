Research directions

Better evidence, not a louder claim.

Four hypotheses, ranked qualitatively by potential contribution and cost. None was run or assigned as the session’s method lane; their uniqueness beyond this empty starting repo is unproven.

Prioritized hypotheses

What might reveal a missing fault?

01 · Offset magnetic–scarp agreement / medium cost

Compare multi-scale magnetic edges with signed LiDAR crest-to-toe asymmetry, allowing a bounded cross-strike offset. A buried displacement might produce both signals; lithologic contacts, shorelines and survey seams can mimic them.

02 · Deflected drainage and matched scarp / high cost

Use 1 m DEM-derived channel geometry and opposite fan surfaces, rather than slope alone. Erosion, avulsion and terrace risers are non-fault alternatives. Complete, authorized DEM tiles are not available here.

03 · Thermal–chemical residual near an independent break / medium–high cost

Combine temperature probes and well/spring chemistry with independent radiometric and magnetic structure, controlling observation density and access. Lateral groundwater flow or preferential sampling can mimic the signal.

04 · Multi-depth edge displacement / high cost

Compare gravity, magnetics and depth-dependent conductance after matching resolution. Basin fill or volcanic contacts can produce similar contrasts. The organizer stack and external binaries have not been obtained.

How we will test

Use the unmodified shared evaluator and writer, hide whole fault components with a spatial buffer, derive catalogue features only from visible faults, mask those visible pixels exactly, and compute pooled distance-weighted Tversky with a 300 m triangular kernel. Check every feature as a leakage canary; compare with a genuinely equivalent baseline and report a spatial-block confidence interval.

The literal registry tests happen before placement and again on the final dots. Stop at the first duplicate; do not exclude inconvenient controls or tune a model around them.

Evidence discipline

What a fault score is not

A local catalogue-recovery holdout is not a leaderboard estimate. A fault prediction is not confirmation of a geothermal vent or a commercially viable resource. Non-fault mimics and spatial acquisition bias must remain explicit.

The four ideas are proposals, not evidence of success. A fresh method lane, data provenance, a comparable baseline and the full live registry are still needed.
