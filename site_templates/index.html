What matters now

Three reasons to wait.

01 / UNIQUENESS

A registered lattice forces a stop.

A spacing-5 control covers almost the entire mirrored footprint within 3 pixels. At the historical H33 dot count, even the mathematical minimum overlap exceeds the 70% limit. This is a geometry bound, not a score.

02 / DATA INTEGRITY

The mirror is not the official sample.

The peer sample’s finite values exactly match the peer fault labels. It is useful for grid forensics only and must never be used as a prediction or as a model feature.

03 / FORMAT

Range checks are not enough.

The organizer specifies null or NaN outside the data bounds. The reported range error’s cause is unknown; a zero exterior is not proof of organizer acceptance.

The H33 question

A strong result is not a known cause.

The GEMSDOE32 historical raster is exactly the GEMSDOE25 pattern after removing dots within two pixels of the mirrored known-fault catalogue. Sparse predictions can reduce false-positive mass while retaining some distance-weighted credit, but the hidden truth and an artifact-linked score receipt are unavailable.

A better score is possible in principle—the official public leaderboard is higher—but no new method here has demonstrated it. We will not convert a holdout result into a fabricated competition score.
