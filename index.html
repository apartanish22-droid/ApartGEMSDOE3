The result that needs explaining

GEMSDOE32 H33-B2 leads the supplied nine—
but the evidence has limits.

0.2778 OWNER-REPORTED is the highest of the nine owner/user-reported values in the published focus-site audit. No archived organizer submission-page receipt was found for the file, so it is not ORGANIZER-CONFIRMED.

The most plausible metric explanation is sparse pruning: a leaner prediction field may preserve nearby coverage while shedding redundant or low-credit mass under the official distance-weighted Tversky score. That is a mechanism consistent with the reported nested masks—not proof of why a hidden-test score changed or of new fault discovery.

Uniqueness still fails. The owner-side audit reports every historical top mask exceeds the 3-pixel overlap limit against at least one other focus-site raster. We did not replay that comparison locally; no old TIFF is promoted here.
