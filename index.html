Why the reported GEMSDOE32 result?

The historical sequence is consistent with thinning densely spaced predictions and removing points near known faults: both can reduce redundant false-positive mass for a distance-weighted metric. That is a hypothesis about mechanism, not proof of causality or future leaderboard improvement. The reported number is not a validated estimate for a new raster.

How to submit once a candidate is approved

  1. Confirm the current executive summary explicitly says a new file is authorized, with a unique filename, short note, full-registry and fold-local evidence, and selector approval.
  2. Download that GeoTIFF and compare its SHA-256 with its run card. The validator must check saved bytes, a single band, finite values in [0,1], and the organizer template CRS, shape, and transform.
  3. Visit the official competition, sign in, open its submissions page, upload the approved file and enter the short note. Confirm the current weekly limit before using a slot.

These steps are future instructions, not permission to submit either research file above.

Research shortlist

Four untested proposals, ranked qualitatively; none has a verified expected DTI improvement or established uniqueness.

  1. Medium cost: signed DEM cross-profile step versus smooth slope, testing weak range-front scarps. Shoreline terraces and road cuts can mimic the signature.
  2. Medium cost: gravity and magnetic multiscale phase-edge alignment, testing concealed basement contacts; unfaulted lithologic boundaries are a confounder.
  3. High cost: robust multivariate radiometric residuals conditional on terrain, testing alteration corridors; soil and survey seams are confounders.
  4. High cost: paired drainage asymmetry and cross-divide relief, testing long-lived tectonic tilt; lithology and climate can mimic it.

Compare each against existing and peer methods before declaring novelty. The cache and source coverage are not yet verified. Full discussion: research report on GitHub.
