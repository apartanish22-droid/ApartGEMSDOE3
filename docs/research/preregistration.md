No single assigned method paragraph was present in the request; consequently these are proposals, not permission to claim an assigned lane. No experiment or submission slot is authorized by this document. Expected impact is a qualitative hypothesis, not a leaderboard score.

## Frozen validation sequence

Once a lane and actual, authorized inputs exist, first test every raw and derived feature separately using the **shared** evaluator. A feature-only AUC above 0.90 is leakage until independently explained. Hide whole connected fault components with a buffer, build all catalogue-derived inputs from the visible folds only, mask visible fault pixels exactly, and pool TPw/FPw/FNw using alpha 0.2, beta 0.8 and a 300 m triangular kernel. Record a spatial-block 95% CI and withheld positive count. Compare with the best genuinely comparable, fold-local baseline; the un-replayed DPRF1 peer record is not a baseline that this checkout can certify. Before placement compare the surface against the complete immutable registry; after placement check full-footprint rank correlation and candidate-to-reference dot overlap. Stop on the first duplicate. Apply the shared writer and byte-level format validation only if all previous gates pass. A separate selector decides whether a competition slot may be used.

Budget: no more than three experiments or two hours. No calibration against a hidden test set, no use of previous TIFF predictions as a generated candidate, no treatment of proxy DTI as a leaderboard projection, and no exemption for the registered lattice. The official organizer says the target is faults, not confirmation of a vent or a commercial reservoir.

This release notes different acquisition areas, flight specifications, terrain clearance and correction workflows; these motivate nuisance controls, not a claim that controls eliminate all bias.

- [USGS map discussion of offset alluvial channels](https://pubs.usgs.gov/of/2006/1276/of06_1276_pamphlet.pdf): analogous geological evidence, not proof of faults in our study area.
- [INGENIOUS public dataset catalog](https://gdr.openei.org/submissions/1391). The catalog advertises downloadable resources; catalog visibility is not proof the data were downloaded, licensed for a particular reuse, or validated in this run.
