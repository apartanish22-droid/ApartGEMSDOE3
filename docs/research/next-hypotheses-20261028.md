# Candidate research directions — 2026-10-28

These are untested research proposals, not assigned lanes or validated methods. Ranking is qualitative and speculative; no claim of novelty across other projects is made. The missing method paragraph means none is authorized for a parallel run. No extra source has been established as obtainable in this restricted environment. The first two could use a correctly identified cached stack if its underlying layers and availability are verified; the third requires additional data. Recheck prior implementations before preregistration.

The competition targets faults, not direct proof of geothermal vents. All proposals must pass feature-level leakage canaries, visible-only buffered segment holdouts, the literal pre/post-placement registry gates, comparison with a matching incumbent, and separate slot selection. AUC and holdout metrics measure proxies; no live score may be projected from them without validated calibration.
