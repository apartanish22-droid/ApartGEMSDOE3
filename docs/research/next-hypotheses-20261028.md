# Candidate research directions — 2026-10-28 (ranked)

These are untested research proposals, not assigned lanes or validated methods. Ranking is qualitative and speculative; no claim of novelty across other projects is made. None is authorized for a parallel run until it passes feature-level leakage canaries, a visible-only buffered segment holdout, the literal pre/post-placement registry gates, a comparison with a matching incumbent, and a separate slot-selection decision. AUC and holdout metrics measure proxies; no live score may be projected from them without validated calibration.

The competition targets faults, not direct proof of geothermal vents. The hypothesis that scored highest among the nine focus sites is GEMSDOE32 `h33-h33-2-b2-20261004T220000Z-e5eb6e7e-zeros` (owner-report 0.2778), a dotted, catalogue-proximate submission. The proposals below deliberately hunt for faults MISSING from the USGS/INGENIOUS catalogue (off-catalogue / blind), because duplicating catalogue-proximate dots is the duplicate-lane trap.

## Recommendation
Validate hypothesis 2 (post-2021 ComCat + radiometric) on the spatially-blocked holdout first: it is label-free, uses a restored official source, targets post-catalogue faulting (highest chance of finding faults the catalogue misses), and is cheap to implement. If its single-feature canary AUC exceeds 0.90, treat as leakage and reject. Only after it beats the current holdout best on a comparable metric should a weekly-slot selector be engaged.
