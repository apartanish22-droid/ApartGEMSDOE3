## H33 / reported 0.2778: what can and cannot be concluded

The user identifies H33-2-B2 as scoring 0.2778. The separate public [GEMSDOE32 PR #9](https://github.com/buffedlizard55-lab/GEMSDOE32/pull/9) describes the H33-2-B2 *mechanism* as pruning the B=2 catalogue flank from a 40,199-dot base to 37,654 dots (removing 2,545 predictions at the specified catalogue buffer). Its PR description reports a local live-mirror change of +0.004870 in 4/4 folds and a safety factor of 2.08, and gives a **projected** 0.2747. Those are repository-reported calculations/projections, not organizer-confirmed score receipts.

A plausible scientific explanation is that suppressing predictions close to already mapped faults reduces false-positive mass in areas where mapped faults are not the target, while retaining enough spatial coverage of unmapped fault candidates. A sparse ranked prediction can outperform a broader map under an asymmetric detection metric if the removed mass is mostly uncredited. However, the public PR itself says no organizer score was present in that repository and notes disagreement between holdout evaluators for the historical champion. Therefore the causal explanation for the stated 0.2778 is **not established here**; neither exact-score provenance nor a valid uncertainty interval is available. It is not possible to claim that a new submission can beat 0.2778, 0.3195, or the current leaderboard leader on this evidence.

The upstream PR is a useful lead, not authority for the leaderboard. Verify any score directly against a submission-page receipt or organizer leaderboard. The user's numbers and the repository's projection must not be relabeled `ORGANIZER-CONFIRMED`.
