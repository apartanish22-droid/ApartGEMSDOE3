Holdout design

Hide, reconstruct, compare

Withhold whole segments with a spatial buffer; rebuild catalogue-derived features from visible faults only; mask visible faults pixel-exactly; score pooled distance-weighted Tversky using the shared evaluator. Test every feature alone; an AUC above 0.90 is a leakage stop until resolved. Compare against a frozen current best on identical folds using paired spatial confidence intervals.

Registry design

Check before and after placement

Stop on rank-correlation above 0.90 or more than 70% of candidate dots within three pixels of any registry raster. Do not silently exempt the lattice or a continuous raster. The present registry is incomplete, and a budget-specific counting bound blocks the common 37,654-dot design.

What is the scientific distinction?

GeoDAWN's magnetic data inform subsurface structure while radiometrics provide surface-geology information. That makes cross-modal consistency a reasonable test, not proof of a fault. A local catalogue holdout tests recovery of mapped examples and may not transfer to blind faults selected by expert interpreters. USGS GeoDAWN data release ↗
